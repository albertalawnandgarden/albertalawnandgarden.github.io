Established in 2004

Experience rooted in Alberta

Alberta Lawn & Garden was established in 2004. Owner Eric Oosterhuis is a graduate of the University of Alberta's Agriculture faculty. He worked for eight years as a research agronomist with Alberta Agriculture, promoting direct-seeding techniques, then served as Agricultural Superintendent for Agrium Inc. before starting the business.

Based in St. Albert, Alberta Lawn & Garden provides landscaping and lawn maintenance during the warmer months, as well as snow removal throughout the winter, serving clients in St. Albert and Edmonton.
